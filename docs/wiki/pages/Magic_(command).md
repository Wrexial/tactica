# Magic (command)

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_%28command%29
> Group: Gameplay

*Squall using Magic in Final Fantasy VIII.*

Magic (まほう, Mahō?), also known as Spell, is a recurring command in the Final Fantasy series that usually appears in every battle event. When selected, a menu will appear, listing all usable magic spells. Magic is commonly cast by expending MP.

When a player character is afflicted with the Silence status effect, the Magic command is inaccessible. Certain titles in the series also disable the Magic command when a player enters certain boss battles.

An upgrade to the Magic command is the Dualcast, which allows a character to cast two spells at once.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy*

Main article: Magic (Final Fantasy)

Magic has two categories; White and Black. Low level White Magic is usable by Knights, and mid level White Magic is usable by the Red Mage/Wizard, while higher levels are restricted to the White Mage/Wizard. Low level Black Magic is usable by Ninjas, and mid level Black Magic is usable by the Red Mage/Wizard, while higher levels are restricted to the Black Mage/Wizard. Magic must be bought in shops before it can be used.

In the NES version Magic can only be cast for a limited number of times depending on their spell level, but since the Dawn of Souls version, spells have used the MP system instead.

### *Final Fantasy II*

Main article: Magic (Final Fantasy II)

*Magic command (GBA).*

There are 40 spells in total; 20 White and 20 Black. Anyone can learn any spell as it is not based on class. When the player teaches a character a new spell it starts at level 1 and rises in level the more it is used in battle. The max level a spell can be raised to is level 16.

A trick to raise spells quickly is to get in battle with a weak enemy and cast the spells one wants to raise 100 times, as doing this will ensure the spell will rise a level. There is also a target-cancel bug in some versions that lets the player level up their spells by repeatedly selecting and cancelling the Magic command.

In the iOS version, the "Magic Command" can cast a spell on a single target or on an entire group. When choosing who to cast the spell on the player must double-tap a target to select them. To select the entire group (of enemies or player party) the player must feather swipe from the bottom right to the top left of the group. This means a quick motion that starts and ends with the finger in the air.

### *Final Fantasy III*

Magic is split into three categories; Summon Magic, Black Magic, and White Magic. Summon magic is usable by Evoker, Summoner, and Sage jobs.

Black Magic is usable by Freelancer, Black Mage, Red Mage, Magus, Sage, and Onion Knight. White Magic is usable by Freelancer, White Mage, Red Mage, Knight, Devout, Sage, and Onion Knight jobs.

### *Final Fantasy IV*

*The party casts magic.*

Magic is divided into six categories, which include White Magic, Black Magic, Summon Magic ("Call" on the SNES), Twincast, Item magic, and a special type of offensive and support magic used by Edge known as Ninjutsu ("Ninja" on the SNES). Twincast uses spells that are not present in any other types of magic, while Item magic includes new spells along with existing spells found within the other types. Eight of the twelve playable characters can use magic (the exceptions being Kain, Edward, Yang, and Cid), and they gain magic spells at pre-programmed experience levels or fixed story events. Recall is another type of magic used by Tellah that casts forgotten spells.

In the 3D versions, Dualcast is an Augment ability compatible with all forms of magic.

#### *Final Fantasy IV -Interlude-*

There are six magic categories: White Magic, Black Magic, Summon Magic, Ninjutsu, Twincast, and item magic.

#### *Final Fantasy IV: The After Years*

There are five magic categories: White Magic, Black Magic, Summon Magic, Ninjutsu, and item magic. Twincast is not a skillset anymore, but a Band.

### *Final Fantasy V*

*Bartz casts magic as a White Mage.*

Magic is divided into skillsets accessed by equipping the following command abilities:

- !White Magic
- !Black Magic
- !Summon (or its probabilistic equivalent !Call)
- !Red Magic
- !Time Magic
- !Blue Magic
- !Spellblade
- !Sing
- !Gaia
- !Animals
- !Mix (partially)
- !Dance (partially)
- !Combine
- !Predict
- !Condemn
- !Dark Arts
- !Oath

The last five are only available in the Advance and discontinued 2013 mobile and Steam versions. Each skillset is native to a job (e.g. White Magic is native to the White Mage job).

Some command abilities have an internal tier system based on the job level of their associated job. While a job that natively uses a magic skillset can access the full spell list, characters using the corresponding command ability from another job can only cast spells up to the level unlocked by the native job's progression.

White, Black, and Time Magic spells are purchased in various shops throughout the game. Some can also be found earlier via treasure chests or environmental interactions. Level 1 Summon spells are also shop-bought, while stronger summons require either scripted events or defeating the corresponding boss in battle. Blue Magic is learned from enemies during combat. Red Magic and Spellblade spells are automatically acquired alongside their respective White and Black Magic counterparts. Songs are found by interacting with specific NPCs or field elements. Gaia and Animals spells become available as the character's level increases. Mix, Dance, Combine, Predict, and Condemn spells are all available as soon as the corresponding command abilities are learned. Dark Arts are unlocked by having a Necromancer defeat specific enemies.

Some spells can also be cast as item magic and/or added abilities of certain weapons. The player does not need to have learned the original versions of such spells in order to cast them via item magic and added abilities.

The Red Mage can learn Dualcast, allowing the character to cast two spells in one turn. Dualcast works with the first five skillsets listed above, provided they are equipped. It also grants access to the first three levels of White and Black Magic by default.

Spells either always hit or are subject to hit determination. For these purposes, spells are classified as either magical spells or physical spells. All spells are also assigned an action category.

Spells that deal damage fall into one of several categories:

- Regular spells
- Defense-piercing spells
- Defense-ignoring spells
- Purely random spells
- Physical spells
- Predict spells
- Level-based magical spells
- Goblin Punch

### *Final Fantasy VI*

Main article: Magic (Final Fantasy VI command)

The Magic command is initially only available to Terra and Celes. After the party acquires their first espers, every playable character, except for Umaro and Gogo, can learn magic by equipping espers. Gogo can still use the Magic command by mimicking other party members. Dualcast can be used if a character is equipped with the Soul of Thamasa relic; the only drawback is that the character cannot summon the esper he or she currently has equipped.

### *Final Fantasy VII*

*Cid casting magic.*

Main article: Magic (Final Fantasy VII)

The Magic command is available to anyone equipped with Magic Materia, and enough MP to cast at least one of the spells they know—for example, if a character knows a spell using 30 MP, and only has 20 MP, the Magic command is disabled. The Magic command can be changed to W-Magic with the Command Materia of same name. By linking Magic Materia with Support Materia the player can get different results when using the command, such as using a spell that is linked to an All Materia will result in the spell hitting all targets.

The formula for magic damage is as follows:

`Base~Magic~Damage=6\times [Level+Magic]`

#### *Dirge of Cerberus -Final Fantasy VII-*

Main article: Magic (Dirge of Cerberus)

Magic can be used when the appropriate key is pressed with appropriate equipment equipped. There are three varieties of Magic Materia that grant spells. Further equipment increase the level of spell, which makes them more effective for a greater MP cost.

Though the spells are named after historically elemental spells, there is no element system and the spells function differently in regards to behavior, area of effect, damage, and MP cost.

#### *Crisis Core -Final Fantasy VII-*

While there is no Magic command, Zack can use magic in battle by equipping magic materia and expending MP to cast it. Abilities are not divided in skillsets, but different attacks/spells become available depending on which Materia Zack has equipped.

#### *Final Fantasy VII Remake*

Main article: Magic (VII Remake)

Spells are used with the Magic command, available to anyone with ![Magic Materia] Magic Materia equipped who is not incapacitated by spell-preventing statuses. Using spells requires MP and ATB charges, stronger spells requiring more of both. Magic materia can be linked with various ![Support Materia] Support Materia to enhance their effect. Spells cast on Arcane Ward are cast twice. Spells that target an enemy's elemental weakness are typically excellent for pressuring foes.

#### *Final Fantasy VII Rebirth*

Main article: Magic (VII Rebirth)

The Magic command returns, unchanged from Remake, though the player has more ![Magic Materia] Magic Materia and ![Support Materia] Support Materia at their disposal and some summons can use the same spells the player gets from magic materia. Arcane Ward returns, and Yuffie can additionally use Doppelgänger to doublecast spells.

### *Final Fantasy VIII*

Main article: Magic (Final Fantasy VIII)

Magic is a junction ability every Guardian Force has already mastered. It may be removed using the item Amnesia Greens, and later re-added using the Magic Scroll item bought from the pet shop in Timber. To use the command, the player must acquire some spells by drawing them from enemies and draw points, or by refining from items. The game uses no MP and instead spells are stocked and used up individually. A total of thirty-two different spells can be stocked on a given character.

Magic is not as powerful when used via Magic command, but is more useful for junctioning to stats. Selphie's and Rinoa's Limit Breaks use the same magic as from the Magic command, but Selphie can cast multiple spells at once via Slot and Rinoa's Angel Wing spells are four times as powerful as normal spells.

### *Final Fantasy IX*

*Dagger casting magic.*

Magic is divided into three categories: Black Magic, White Magic, and Blue Magic. Vivi has Black Magic, and Dagger and Eiko, as well as the temporary character Beatrix, have White Magic, whereas Quina learns Blue Magic. When Steiner and Vivi are in the active party, they can combine their powers in Sword Magic techniques.

Magic is usually learned from equipment, but Quina learns Blue Magic by eating enemies. During Trance, Vivi's Black Magic becomes Dbl Blk, and Eiko's White Magic becomes Dbl Wht.

In the Japanese version, the SFX command is called "Magic" (まほう, Mahō?) in hiragana (other magic commands use kanji, i.e. 魔法?). The abilities in this skillset are not actually flagged as magic, and always inflict 0 damage.

Formula for Magic:

`Base=Spell~Power-Target's~Mag~Defense`

  `Bonus = Magic + RndMOD ([(Level + Magic) / 8] + 1)`

  `Damage = Base \times Bonus`

### *Final Fantasy X*

*Lulu prepares to cast Ultima.*

See also: Wht Magic (Final Fantasy X) or Blk Magic (Final Fantasy X)

Anyone who has learned either a White or Black Magic spell via the Sphere Grid has the appropriate command added to their Command Menu to use the spell in battle. Most Black Magic is located on Lulu's path on the Sphere Grid, and most White Magic is located on Yuna's path, although Tidus learns spells that manipulate the turn order.

The formula for determining how much damage a Magic spell will do is as follows:

`Damage=Spell~Power\times ((Magic^{2}/6)+Spell~Power)/4`

The formula for determining how much a spell will heal for is as follows:

`Healing=Spell~Power\times (Magic+Spell~Power)/2`

Magic can also be taught to aeons.

#### *Final Fantasy X-2*

See also: Black Magic (Final Fantasy X-2), White Magic (Final Fantasy X-2), Blue Bullet, or Arcana (Final Fantasy X-2)

What commands a character has depends on which dressphere she is wearing and which Garment Grid she is equipping. Black Mage and White Mage learn the Black and White Magic commands, respectively, and learn more spells by leveling up their individual dressphere.

Certain spells, such as Holy, can only be acquired through certain Garment Grids. It is also possible to use magic outside of their originating dressphere. For example: Equipping the accessory Black Lore allows the use of Black Magic spells learned as a Black Mage.

Blue Bullet is the equivalent of Blue Magic. The Arcana skillset casts status ailments and is associated with the Dark Knight dressphere.

### *Final Fantasy XI*

There are seven main categories of spells that are executed using commands from the magic menu: Black Magic, White Magic, Bard Songs, Ninjutsu, Summoning Magic, Blue Magic, and Geomancy.

All magic falls under a certain skill category: White Magic and Black Magic both include Enhancing Magic and Enfeebling Magic. Black Magic also includes Elemental Magic and Dark Magic, and White Magic also includes Divine Magic and Healing Magic.

All Blue Magic is attributed to the Blue Magic skill, all Ninjutsu is under the Ninjutsu skill category, and all summoning spells are under the Summoning Magic skill category. Bard songs are broken down into three magic skill types: Singing, String Instrument, and Wind Instrument. Geomancy is attributed to Geomancy and Handbell skills.

An eighth category known as Trust Magic also exists but is executed from its own command in the menu. It allows players to summon NPC allies to fight alongside them, and are only affected by a player's level.

### *Final Fantasy XII*

See also: White Magicks (Final Fantasy XII), Black Magicks (Final Fantasy XII), Green Magicks (Final Fantasy XII), Time Magicks (Final Fantasy XII), or Arcane Magicks (Final Fantasy XII)

*Balthier casts Faith.*

Magicks and Technicks command opens a submenu listing the types of magick spells or Technicks the player has obtained and unlocked on the License Board. Individual spells/technicks must be purchased in shops/found in treasures before they can be used. Anyone can use any spell after acquiring its license in the original PlayStation 2 version, but in the Zodiac versions, only certain jobs learn magick, such as Black Mage and White Mage. Many spells were switched to different skillsets in this version as well.

Every spell has the same Charge Time, but with the battle mode set to "Wait" no other action can take place while a spell animation is happening, thus using spells with longer animations slows the party down. Even if the battle mode is set to "Active", how many spells can overlap is determined by the effect capacity; when the capacity is full, other spells must "wait for their turn". The most powerful spells hog the whole capacity in the PlayStation 2 versions, so only one such spell can be cast at any one time. The effect capacity is much greater in The Zodiac Age versions, as the game is no longer being held back by the technical limitations of the PlayStation 2.

The formula for damage is:

`DMG=[POW\times RANDOM(1~1.125)-MDEF]\times [2+MAG\times (Lv+MAG)/256)]`

The formula for healing is:

`HEAL=POW\times RANDOM(1~1.125)\times [2+MAG\times (Lv+MAG)/256)]`

Healing does not consider the target's magick defense and the same formula applies when healing magicks are targeted to enemy creatures, such as the undead. The Syphon spell also applies the heal formula, which ignores enemy magick defense.

The damage inflicted with magick is further affected by the character's status (Faith boosts magick power), what augments they have purchased on the License Board, the elemental affinity of their equipment and the enemies' possible elemental weaknesses, as well as the weather and terrain. Some Espers can also use magick. After using a magick spell, there is a small delay until the caster's ATB bar starts to refill, which can compound during boss battles. The Zodiac versions' removal of the damage limit makes using magick more useful overall, and also the boosts to magick power earned from licenses and some accessories worthwhile.

The Sage's Ring accessory halves the MP cost of spells for the wearer. The Turtleshell Choker makes the wearer expend gil rather than MP to use magick. After the original Japanese version, spells no longer consume MP if they do not connect with their target.

#### *Final Fantasy XII: Revenant Wings*

Magic is not a separate command, but every individual spell is its own command. New magick is unlocked by reaching pre-programmed level caps. Kytes learns offensive magick, such as Thundaga and Flare, and Penelo learns healing magick such as Curaga and Esuna, and the one damage-dealing spell, Holy.

### *Final Fantasy XIII*

*Ruin spell cast by Commando.*

The characters don't have a separate Magic command, but all abilities are listed under the Abilities command. What magic a character can cast in battle depends on their current role and how far they have developed in their Crystarium. Everybody's Crystarium development is unique, and one character never learns every skill.

Commando learns only the non-elemental magic spells Ruin and Ruinga (Final Fantasy XIII). Ravager learns elemental magic, such as Fire and Blizzard. Medic learns healing magic, such as Cure and Esuna. Synergist learns enhancing magic, such as Protect and Haste. Saboteur learns debilitating magic, such as Slow and Poison. The only role that has no magic is Sentinel.

Magic cannot be used outside of battle, and the party is healed after every battle. Spell "cost" is measured in ATB segments; low level spells take one ATB segment to execute, whereas powerful spells takes three segments.

Formula for damaging spells:

`Damage=User's~Magic\times Spell~Power`

Formula for healing spells:

`Cure=(User's~Magic\times 0.2)+100`

  `Cura=(User's~Magic\times 0.18)+100`

  `Curasa=(Target's~Missing~HP\times 0.15)+((Target's~Missing~HP\times User's~Magic)/100,000)`

  `Curaja=(Target's~Missing~HP\times 0.12)+((Target's~Missing~HP\times User's~Magic)/100,000)`

#### *Final Fantasy XIII-2*

The system is identical to Final Fantasy XIII, but now the party has Paradigm Pack allies, each with a single role, and thus the third party member in a formation changes depending on the paradigm in use. Commando casts non-elemental magic. Ravager casts elemental magic. Medic casts curative magic. Synergist casts status enhancements. Saboteur casts status ailments. Sentinels don't use magic. Lightning has special roles in the Lightning's Story: Requiem of the Goddess DLC scenario.

#### *Lightning Returns: Final Fantasy XIII*

*Lightning casts Fira.*

See also: Lightning Returns: Final Fantasy XIII abilities

There is no distinction between magic and any other ability Lightning can use. Magic is cast by assigning different buttons from the right panel of the pad. Magic is dropped by enemies in the form of crystal or per-assigned to garbs. Synthesizing magic of the same element produces stronger magic spells, and after a certain day, spells can be upgraded. Magic is often superior to physical attacks for staggering opponents.

### *Final Fantasy XIV*

Magic abilities are categorized as spells. Spells frequently consume MP, often have a cast time, and are disabled by the Silence condition. They are primarily, but not exclusively, used by Disciples of Magic.

Spells are found as part of combo chains less frequently than weaponskills. Instead, it is more common for spells to trigger an additional effect if certain conditions are met (or purely by random chance). For example, a Paladin's Clemency spell triggers an additional healing effect on the Paladin if cast on another party member.

### *Final Fantasy XV*

*Noctis casts a fire spell, setting the surrounding field ablaze.*

See also: Elemancy or Ring of the Lucii

There are two types of magic: one can only be used by Noctis via the Ring of the Lucii, and is not available at the beginning, but will be unlocked throughout the story. The Ring of the Lucii allows Noctis to cast Death, Holy, and Alterna. Depending on the spell used, differing amounts of MP are consumed. The MP stat is exclusive to Noctis, who can also use it to warp.

The other form of magic is elemancy that does not require MP, and is not exclusive to Noctis. Elemancy is crafted in Magic Flasks after collecting the ingredients from the environments, or by defeating enemies with certain weapons. Three uses of a spell can be equipped in a Magic Flask, which then needs to be equipped as a Primary Arm (for Noctis), or a Secondary Arm (for party members). To use different spells in battle, multiple flasks will need to be equipped. Spells can be crafted during battle.

Casting spells has residual effects come into effect in real time. Weather has a tactical impact on battle conditions, with different types of magic having varying effects depending on the weather. This reinforces the tactical and situational use of magic, as party members are not immune to the residual effects, e.g. using fire spells on a clear day not only hurts monsters, but also ignites the surrounding area; monsters afraid of fire will be sent fleeing, but it can hurt party members as well, unless the Ring of Resistance is equipped. Fire is extinguished fast if it rains, but thunder magic is augmented during this time. Though magic can destroy the environment, the effects will be slowly restored. When Noctis uses elemancy, the player must manually aim it. When equipped to party members, the AI will use it at its own discretion, usually only against tougher foes.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics*

Magicks are divided and associated in each of their corresponding jobs: White Magicks, Black Magicks, Time Magicks, and Summon. Spells are considered Action Abilities and must be purchased with JP, or earned from a deceased unit's crystal, before being able to be used in battle.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

The base damage formula for Magical abilities are as follows:

`Caster's~Magic~Power-(Target's~Magic~Resistance/2)`

Afterwards, damage will be multiplied by Power/100, where Power is usually equal to the strength of the ability being used. For weapon-based abilities, this will be the EqAtt bonus the unit's equipment (minus secondary weapons) gives, For other abilities, this will be the Power stat of the ability itself.

#### *Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift*

Various Magic classes have their own set of commands for magic. The White Mage has access to White Magick, Black Mage to Black Magick, Seer to High Magick, Red Mage to Red Magick, Green Mage to Green Magick, Blue Mage to Blue Magick, Time Mage to Time Magick, Bishop to Intercession, Alchemist for Alchemy, Sage for Sagacity, and Illusionist for Illusion.

### *Final Fantasy Mystic Quest*

Main article: Spell (Mystic Quest)

The command is called Spell. There are three classes of magic, White, Black, and Wizard. Each type has their own set amount of uses before the character cannot use anymore spells of that type, but all types are automatically restored when the character has his or her MP restored.

White Magic includes Cure, Exit, Heal, and Life. Black Magic spells include Quake, Fire, Blizzard, and Aero. Wizard Magic includes the powerful spells Thunder, Holy, Meteor, and Flare.

### *Final Fantasy Legend III*

The battle command is Magic, and all three types of magic can be found within this command. These three types of magic are White Magic, Black Magic, and Lost Magic. Up to 24 magic spells of any type can be equipped on a single character.

## Etymology

Magic or sorcery is an attempt to understand, experience and influence the world using rituals, symbols, actions, gestures and language that are believed to exploit supernatural forces.

A spell, charm or hex is a set of words, spoken or unspoken, which are considered by its user to invoke some magical effect. Historical attestations exist for the use of some variety of incantations in many cultures around the world.
