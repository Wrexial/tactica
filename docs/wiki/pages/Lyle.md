Lyle is a resident of St. Ivalice in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. Lyle attends the same school where Marche Radiuju enrolled after moving into town, where he is the PE Head—a student, usually a boy, who cleans up after a PE lesson.

Lyle's clothing resembles the Gaia Gear, a piece of clothing that grants the Auto Regen ability to Snipers, Hunters and Gadgeteers.

## Story

*Lyle and the other students in a snowball fight.*

Lyle is in cohorts with two other kids, Guinness and Colin. The three are school bullies who enjoy taunting Mewt Randell for his shy, odd demeanor, and Ritz Malheur over her dyed hair. During the snowball "fight", the bullies gang up on Mewt. Mr. Leslaie, the "Master" of the fight, sees this and sends them off to his office.

When St. Ivalice is transformed by the Gran Grimoire, Marche and Clan Nutsy accept the quest "Prof in Trouble" to save the nu mou Professor Auggie in Lutia Pass, where the player discovers the three bullies have been turned into Zombies and had been attacking people. Marche does not acknowledge this, and he and his clan defeat the undead bullies. After the main game, Lyle and his friends re-emerge as Vampires in the mission "Mortal Snow", where the clan will recruit Ritz.

When St. Ivalice reverts to its initial state, so do the three bullies, and Marche and Mewt have gathered courage to go against them.
