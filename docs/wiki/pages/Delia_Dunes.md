> Description

Delia Dunes (デライラ砂丘, Deraira sakyū?) is a desert location in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. It can be placed on the World Map after "Mission #017: Desert Patrol" is completed and is the site of the storyline "Mission #018: Quiet Sands".

Delia Dunes is also the site of "Mission #034: Magewyrm", "Mission #066: A Dragon's Aid", and "Mission #091: Challengers?". It can be freed in "Mission #254: Delia Dunes".

## Story

In "Mission #018: Quiet Sands", Marche is confronted by Llednar Twem. A seam is also located here, and Marche and Llednar were teleported to the shrine of the fifth Crystal, the Human Crystal. When Llednar attempts to use his Omega skill, Judgemaster Cid intervenes and sends Llednar to prison. Marche is then free to battle the Human Totema, Mateus.
