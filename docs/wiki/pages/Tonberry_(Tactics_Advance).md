# Tonberry (Tactics Advance)

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Tonberry_%28Tactics_Advance%29
> Group: Enemies

> Green, hooded monster. Carries a wicked knife.
>
>
> Description

The Tonberry in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a small green lizard-like creature that wears a hood and carries a knife and a lantern. Though its Move and Jump stats are low, the Tonberry is dangerous if it can execute its skills on an unsuspecting party member.

See also: Tonberry
