# Final Fantasy Tactics Advance reaction abilities

> Group: Abilities
> Redirect aliases: Absorb MP (Tactics Advance), Auto Regen (Tactics Advance), Block Arrows, Bonecrusher (Tactics Advance), Catch (Tactics Advance), Counter (Tactics Advance), Dragonheart (Tactics Advance), Last Berserk (Tactics Advance), Last Haste (Tactics Advance), Last Quicken (Tactics Advance), Reflex (Tactics Advance), Return Fire (Tactics Advance), Return Magic (Tactics Advance), Strikeback (Tactics Advance)

Reaction abilities (リアクションアビリティ, Riakushon Abiriti?) in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance are learned via Ability Points (AP). Each character can equip one reaction ability. Reaction abilities activate when their trigger condition is met. The reaction abilities vary, ranging from retaliating actions to bestowing positive status.
