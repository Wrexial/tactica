# Final Fantasy Tactics Advance action abilities

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Fantasy_Tactics_Advance_action_abilities
> Group: Abilities

Action abilities (アクションアビリティ, Akushon Abiriti?) in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance are combat abilities learned via Ability Points (AP). The unit's mandatory job ability appears in the top slot, while the second slot may be assigned any action ability the unit has learned.

The following lists action abilities and also includes the flavor text along with the abilities found within their sub-menu, if applicable.

## Action abilities

### Basic

| Fight | Fight with bare hands or a weapon, if equipped. | — | All |

| Combo | All allies with combo abilities attack together. | — | All |
