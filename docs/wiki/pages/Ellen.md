Ellen is a viera from Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. Ellen is the only named shopkeeper. She's the flirtatious owner of the item shop in Muscadet who gives good deals on greatswords. She's also mentioned in the mission "No Answers" along with a member from Clan Ritz.
