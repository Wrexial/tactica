Move is an integral and important stat in the Tactics series of Final Fantasy games. It is the stat that determines how far a unit can travel from their current position, and works in tangent with the Jump statistic, in scenarios in which the player can or cannot cross over tiles lower or higher than the spot they are at. All job classes have a set Move counter, which can further be increased either by abilities or pieces of equipment.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy Tactics*

> A combination of the unit's base movement range and any job, equipment, or ability bonuses. The higher the number, the farther the unit can move.
>
>
> Description (PSP)

"Move" or "Movement" is the stat that determines how far a unit can move on their turn. The base movement is determined by the unit's job, and can be increased further via equipment like battle boots and Germinas boots, and via the movement abilities Movement +1, +2, and +3. Terrain may give movement penalties, such as when wading through water without Swim.
