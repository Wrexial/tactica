# Bomb (Tactics Advance)

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Bomb_%28Tactics_Advance%29
> Group: Enemies

> Fiery, explosive creature. Deals fire damage.
>
>
> Description

Bomb in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a flaming creature that can self-destruct to do massive damage. Being of the Fire element, it is weak to Ice and Water-based attacks. It is related to the Ice-elemental Grenade enemy.

If Firewyrms accompany Bombs, such as in "Mission #047: Hot Recipe", it is best to kill the wyrms because they may use their Fire Breath to deal damage to the party and heal an injured Bomb at the same time.

See also: Bomb (creature)
