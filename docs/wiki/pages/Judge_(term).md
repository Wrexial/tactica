# Judge (term)

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Judge_%28term%29
> Group: Core

*The Archadian Judges from Final Fantasy XII.*

The Judges are a recurring element in the Final Fantasy titles that exist in the Ivalice universe. Generally, Judges are the keepers of law and have held this position since their initial appearance in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy XI*

The in-game moderators, Game Masters (or GM's), appeared in the form of Judges, and were capable of using exclusive magic and abilities.

### *Final Fantasy XII*

Main article: Archadian Judges

*Judge Gabranth.*

Judges serve under the Ministry of Law in the Archadian Empire. The highest in rank is the Judge Magisters, who are often accompanied by subordinate Judges. The Judge Magisters are effectively the Empire's Supreme Court.

#### *Final Fantasy XII: Revenant Wings*

Main article: Judge of Wings

*Judge of Wings.*

The Judge of Wings is aptly referred to as a Judge by the sky pirates due to her armor's resemblance to that of Archadian Judges. However, she is not affiliated with Archadia.

#### *Fortress*

*A mysterious Judge.*

A Judge appears in a concept art. He looks suspiciously like Gabranth, although he wields a lance rather than a sword.

### *Final Fantasy XIV*

Game Masters once again take the form of Judges in both the original Final Fantasy XIV and Final Fantasy XIV: A Realm Reborn.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

*A Judge.*

Main article: Judge (Tactics Advance)

The Judges' debut in the series. They serve to uphold the law set by the Ivalice Royal Family, headed by Queen Remedi and administered by Judgemaster Cid Randell.

#### *Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift*

*A Judge.*

Main article: Judge (Tactics A2)

The Judges are given a different persona as well as a different role. They serve Adjudged Clans where they protect them from death as long as they uphold the laws set during engagements.

### *Final Fantasy Type-0*

Main article: The Rursan Arbiter

The Judge is a being who appears during the time of Tempus Finis. Located in Pandæmonium and it will give the "Final Judgement" to the people of Orience.

### *Final Fantasy Explorers*

Three Gartian Empire NPCs, Biggs, Wedge and Colonel Grauser, wear armor identical to the Judges of Ivalice.
