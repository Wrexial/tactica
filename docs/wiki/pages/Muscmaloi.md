# Muscmaloi

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Muscmaloi
> Group: Core

Muscmaloi (ムスクマロイ, Musukamaroi?, lit. Muskmallow) is a type of herb.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

Muscmaloi grows on the Giza Plains. Muscmaloi is described as being "fever-reducing" in the first mission, "Herb Picking". After the mission is completed, Montblanc explains that it grows "all over" and that he used to pick them when he was little.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift*

Muskmallows are grown at Muskmallow Field.

### *Final Fantasy XIV*

Muscmaloi is an herb, with potent Aether-fortifying properties having its cultivation outlawed in Old Sharlayan a few years ago. In the Sage Questline Faldrinet uses the plant, and Thavnairian alchemical ingredients in his procedures to enhance the human body.

## Trivia

- It is possible that the "muscmaloi" of Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a misinterpretation of a Japanese pronunciation of "musk mallow", which is an actual herbal plant of the extensive mallow family.
