# Firewyrm

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Firewyrm
> Group: Enemies

> Giant scaly monster. Breathes blistering fire.
>
>
> Description

The Firewyrm in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is the Fire elemental variant of the dangerously powerful dragon species. Like its relatives, the Icedrake and Thundrake, its stats are superior to almost every other monster. When paired with other Fire-elemental monsters, Firewyrms like to heal its compatriots with Fire Breath. It can also lower the Defense and Magic Resistance of your units, increasing the damage that they receive. Its Guard-Off ability can be learned by the Blue Mage job as Blue Magic.
