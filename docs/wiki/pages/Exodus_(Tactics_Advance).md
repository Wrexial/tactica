Exodus is the totema of the viera in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, and is the fourth totema fought by Marche Radiuju.

Instead of fighting Marche directly, Exodus transports him into a room with several Exodus Fruit, which he must destroy all the while fending off Babus Swain, who is trying to stop him. After being defeated, Exodus can be summoned by any viera member and Ritz of Clan Nutsy. He is based on Exdeath from Final Fantasy V. In Final Fantasy Tactics Advance Radio Edition, Shara summons him against Queen Remedi.

In his in-game appearance, the symbol on each of Exodus's hands is the astrological sign Libra, the scales.

## Gameplay

See also: Exodus Fruit

*Exodus sprite.*

Instead of fighting Exodus directly, Marche must fight the Exodus Fruit. They are incapable of opposing Marche's efforts to destroy them and have low HP, ranging from around 30 to 60. Marche will be under attack from Babus Swain. Once Babus is neutralized, Marche only has to avoid breaking the law while he destroys the Exodus Fruit unopposed. Once all the Exodus Fruits are destroyed, Exodus is defeated.

## Etymology

The Exodus (from Greek ἔξοδος exodos, "going out") is the charter story of Israel; its message being the Israelites were delivered from slavery by God and therefore belong to him through the covenant. It tells of the enslavement of the Israelites in Egypt following the death of Joseph, their departure under the leadership of Moses, the revelations at Sinai, and their wanderings in the wilderness up to the borders of Canaan.
