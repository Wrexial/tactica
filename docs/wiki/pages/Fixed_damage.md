# Fixed damage

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Fixed_damage
> Group: Gameplay

*Excalipoor in Final Fantasy V does 1 fixed damage.*

Fixed damage is a type of damage found throughout the Final Fantasy series. It is often inflicted by an attack that deals a set amount of damage regardless of what buffs the player has or what level they are, usually bypassing everything bar the Invincible status. Fixed damage differs from fractional damage by not depending on the target's HP stat, but is somewhat similar to HP to one abilities. Some recurring attacks of this type are Gil Toss, 1000 Needles, Karma, and Lifebreak.

Fixed damage must not be confused with damage whose multiplier is so high that it consistently hits the damage cap under all circumstances (e.g. Penance's Judgment Day in Final Fantasy X).

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy IV*

Rydia's Bomb summon inflicts damage based on how much HP she has remaining.

### *Final Fantasy V*

The Blue Magic spell 1000 Needles deals exactly 1000 damage to a target.

???? is an enemy ability used by Azulmagia, Kuza Beast, Tyrannosaur, Wild Nakk, Behemoth, Gel Fish, King Behemoth, Mecha Head, Numb Blade, and Tunneller. It deals damage to one target equal to the difference in the user's Max HP and current HP at the cost of 3 MP. The player may also use this ability by catching and releasing a Reflect Mage or a Tunneller during a fight with a Zephyrus.

Excalipoor always deals one damage when used as a weapon.

Devil's Juice mix deals 666 fixed damage to a target.

### *Final Fantasy VI*

Revenge Blast is a Lore spell for Strago that could be learned from Dark Force, Dragon, Garm, Gigantos, Gilgamesh, Glasya Labolas, Kaiser Dragon, and Pandora, although Strago already starts with this spell. It deals non-elemental damage to one target equal to the difference in Strago's Max HP and current HP at the cost of 31 MP.

Traveler is another of Strago's Lore spells, which deals damage to a target equal to the number of steps the party has taken divided by 32. It costs an amount of MP equal to the number of minutes the game has been played divided by 30, but not greater than 255. Gau can also use Traveler as a Rage, and it costs him 0 MP to use. To achieve the max damage of 9999, the player would have to walk 319,968 steps.

10,000 Needles inflicts 1000 damage 10 times to random targets.

### *Final Fantasy VII*

100 Needles inflicts 100 damage, and 1000 Needles used by Cactuer does 1000 damage to a single target. Spiky Hell used by Bloatfloat does 200 damage. Magic Hammer, used by Razor Weed and can be learned as an enemy skill, removes 100 MP from the enemy and adds it to the caster's. ???? is an enemy ability used by Jersey and Behemoth. It inflicts damage equal to the difference of the user's maximum HP and their current HP at the cost of 3 MP. The player can use ???? as an enemy skill if attacked by the skill with the Enemy Skill Materia equipped. The ability is physical-based and thus is affected by Cover. Chocobuckle deals fixed damage determined by how many battles the player has fled from, and Coin deals fixed damage determined by the amount of gil the player throws.

Should a party member land on 7777 HP, they will initiate All Lucky 7s status, forcing them to attack 64 times for 7777 damage each. Afterwards, they can still inflict 7777 damage with attacks. Enemies can fall under this effect as well.

Tonberries can counter with Time Attack, dealing damage based on how long the game has been played. Master Tonberries attack with Everyone's Grudge, dealing damage based on the total number of enemies the target has killed multiplied by ten. Emerald Weapon's Aire Tam Storm inflicts 1111 damage to the party, multiplied by each Materia they have equipped.

#### *Crisis Core -Final Fantasy VII-*

1,000, 10,000, 100,000 Needles, 5-Gallon Can, Gungnir, Kettle, Power Lv. 1-8, Scatter Shot, Washbowl, and Wash Tub all deal fixed damage. Tonberries use Karma to damage Zack based on how many enemies he has killed. In Reunion, Minerva's Ultima and Judgement Arrow attacks deal fixed damage.

Cactuar's Limit Break on Chocobo Mode, 1000 Needles, deals 18 hits of fixed damage to all enemies. The damage dealt for each hit is as follows:

| Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Level 4 | Level 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 300 | 500 | 1000 | 1500 | 2000 |

A Dud will also deal fixed damage to Zack, ranging from 1-1000.

### *Final Fantasy VIII*

Cactuars can use 1000 Needles, displayed with a barrage of "1's", while Jumbo Cactuar's 10,000 needles always inflicts 10,000 damage and is displayed with a barrage of "10's". Everyone's Grudge, used by Tonberry, damages a party member based on the number of enemies they have slain multiplied by 20.

Ultima Weapon and Omega Weapon's Light Pillar always inflicts 9999 damage to a target, while Omega Weapon's Megido Flame deals 9998 damage to the entire party.

When fighting Ultimecia's Griever form and final form, she can deal 9999 damage to a GF that is being summoned.

The Cactuar Guardian Force deals fixed damage depending on his level*100, in multiples of 1000. Gilgamesh's Excalipoor attack always inflicts 1 damage.

The Kamikaze command ability deals five times the user's maximum HP as fixed damage to one target, or double that with a critical hit, up to 60,000 HP damage. The Return Damage support ability returns a quarter of the enemy's damage sustained to the attacker.

### *Final Fantasy IX*

Zidane's Thievery skill inflicts fixed damage based on the number of successful steals. His Lucky Seven skill inflicts 7, 77, 777, or 7777 damage if Zidane's HP currently ends in 7. His Mug makes his successful steals also hurt the target with fixed damage based on his and his target's levels.

Freya's Dragon's Crest inflicts fixed damage dependent on the number of dragons the player has killed. Her Dragon Breath doubles the damage the party has already inflicted onto all enemies.

Quina's Frog Drop deals damage according to how many frogs the player has caught, and their Limit Glove deals 9999 damage if they have exactly 1 HP remaining. Pumpkin Head is a Blue Magic spell that inflicts non-elemental damage to one target equal to the difference in Quina's Max HP and current HP at the cost of 12 MP. Quina can eat a Cactuar to gain the Blue Magic move 1,000 Needles.

Minus Strike is an ability usable by Steiner that deals non-elemental damage equal to the difference in Steiner's Max HP and current HP at the cost of 8 MP.

Using a Dark Matter in battle deals 9999 damage to a target. Using a Dead Pepper as an offensive item in battle deals fixed damage to a target depending on how many peppers the player is currently holding in their inventory.

Cactuars can counter with 1000 Needles. Tonberries' Chef's Knife deals 9999 damage to a party member, and Everyone's Grudge inflicts damage on the party based on how many Tonberries have been felled.

### *Final Fantasy X*

Spare Change damages an enemy for the result of dividing the amount of gil spent by 10. Rikku's Quartet of 9 and Trio of 9999 mixes set all healing and damage values to 9999, the former mix targeting a random party member and the latter targeting everyone. This only takes effect if the healing or damage would have been at least 1, and it is still possible to deal over 9999 damage with attacks that break the damage limit. The Sunburst mix also deals exactly 19,998 damage to all enemies.

Qactuars attack with 1000 Needles, while Cactuars attack with 10,000 Needles, and the Cactuar King attacks with 10,000 and 99,999 Needles. An aeon's Shield will reduce the damage of 1000 Needles and 10,000 Needles to 250 and 2,500 respectively, while Boost will increase it 1,500 and 15,000 respectively. Although 99,999 Needles would actually deal 509,949 damage, it is inhibited by the 99,999 damage limit, though it is also too powerful to be mitigated.

Self-Destruct deals fixed fire-elemental damage when used by some enemies: Bomb (600 or 1,000), Grenade (600) and Puroboros (2,500). With Shield, it is reduced to 150, 250, or 625, while Boost increases it to 900, 1,500, or 3,750.

Abyss Worm and Geosgaeno can swallow a character. After swallowing a character and being targeted twice, Abyss Worm will use Regurgitate, dealing 10,000 damage to the swallowed character. If Geosgaeno is defeated while it swallowed a character, it will deal 9,999 points of damage to all party members.

Tonberry and Master Tonberry use Karma, dealing damage equal to the number of enemies the target has slain times 10, whereas Don Tonberry's Karma is instead multiplied by 100. They use Voodoo against aeons, dealing 10,000 damage when used by Tonberry or Master Tonberry, though it effectively deals 9,999 damage as it does not break the damage limit. With Shield, the damage is reduced to 2,500.

Zanmato in each of the boss fights against Yojimbo functions similarly to Voodoo, though it can be used against the party and damages all targets.

Yu Pagodas use Power Wave on the penultimate boss to heal it for 1,500 HP, but if afflicted by Zombie it will take damage instead.

#### *Final Fantasy X-2*

Spare Change is unlocked on the Samurai dressphere, and Momentum deals fixed damage based on the number of enemies the user has defeated. The Gunner's On the Level attack deals damage dealt equal to the user's current level times 16. 1000 Needles can be obtained as a Blue Bullet spell, and Storm Cannon inflicts damage equal to the caster's level times 30. The Berserker's Hurt attack deals damage equal to 37.5% of the user's current HP.

The Cat Nip accessory sets all damage and healing to 9999 for the wielder when their HP is critical. In the HD Remaster versions, it also applies Berserk and Slow, meaning any action other than a basic attack will have to be queued before entering a critical state.

1000 Needles, 3000-Needle Special, and 10000 Needles are enemy attacks. Blinding Meridian, Burning Meridian, Chilly Meridian, Maddening Meridian, Quiet Meridian, and Tingling Meridian all deal 100 damage and are used by Oversouled Cactuars. Detonator and Volcano can explode to deal 1800 and 2800 fire-elemental damage. Nooj's Lightfall deals 5,000 damage to the entire party.

### *Final Fantasy XI*

Certain Gigas family Notorious Monsters use an attack called Mercurial Strike, which at random deals 111, 222, 333, 444, 555, 666, 777, 888, 999, or 1111 damage in an area of effect and has an additional effect that depends on which number was randomly chosen.

1000 Needles appears. 2000 Needles makes an exclusive appearance. Cactrot Rapido uses 10000 Needles. Needles attacks are split between groups.

### *Final Fantasy XII*

Several Technicks deal fixed damage: Traveler damages enemies in a radius based on how many steps the player has taken since last using it; Horology damages enemies in a radius based on the in-game clock's minute position and the user's level; Numerology deals 1 damage to enemies in a radius, which doubles with each successive use, and the counter resetting when it misses; 1000 Needles is also a Technick that inflicts 1000 ranged damage to a single enemy.

The Knot of Rust item deals damage based on the user's max HP, and the Dark Matter item deals damage depending on the amount of Knot of Rusts used. Balance is a magick spell that inflicts damage in an area based on the user's Max HP subtracted by their current HP.

Zodiark's Final Eclipse inflicts 50,000 non-elemental damage to foes, or 60,000 in the Zodiac versions. Shemhazai, Zeromus, and Exodus's abilities also deal fixed damage, as well as Zalera's Condemnation.

The Flowering Cactoid can attack the party with 1000 Needles. The Feral Retriever can attack the party with Balance.

### *Final Fantasy XIII*

Cactuar Prime uses 1000 Needles, while the Gigantuar uses 10,000 Needles, though both attacks can be mitigated.

The Dreadnought's Steam Clean will restore its HP by 1 when used.

#### *Final Fantasy XIII-2*

Giant Cactuar and Gigantuar use 1000 Needles, while Metal Gigantuar uses 10,000 Needles. Metallicactuar's Shadowbind attack deals 100 damage.

Tonberries can use Ultimate Grudge to deal 66,666 damage to the party. Snow's Sovereign Fist deals 99,999 damage to the party.

Gahongas's Delirious Dance deals 1 damage to each of its allies. Gilgamesh's Excalipoor attack will deal 1 damage to a party member.

#### *Lightning Returns: Final Fantasy XIII*

The EP ability Quake deals 9,999 damage to all enemies. Cactair's "Take this! 1,000 Needles!" attack deals 1,000 damage to Lightning, and Cactuars can use 1,000 Needles as well. Each of Ereshkigal's Meteor attacks deal 3,000 damage per meteorite.

The Warrior's Headband accessory increases damage dealt by 10. This can be upgraded to the Warrior's Headband+, increasing damage by 100, and the Rakshasa Ring, increasing damage by 1,000.

Cactair and Last Ones have a special damage threshold, which, if not surpassed, causes attacks to deal 1 damage. Of note, Cactair's is 65,535, causing most attacks to deal 1 damage.

### *Final Fantasy XIV*

A variety of different Needles attacks are featured (100, 1000, 3000, 5000, 10,000, 20,000, 100,000), with damage usually split between targets.

Many enrage attacks deal fixed damage, such as 99,999, 999,999, or even 9,999,999. Though these damage values can usually be mitigated, it does not make them survivable.

### *Final Fantasy XV*

Cactuars' and slactuars' needles always deal 100 damage.

In Episode Ardyn, Ardyn's Daemonifcation skill always inflicts 9999 damage to basic enemies when they are in a vulnerable state.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics*

Invigoration (Drain) and Empowerment (Syphon) absorb a fixed amount against most enemies. Archaeodaemons can use the Lifebreak ability. Beowulf's Vengeance and Cloud's Blade Burst also function the same way. Almagest also has the same effect.

Byblos's Vengeance acts similar to the Lifebreak spell, dealing damage equal to the difference in the user's Max HP and current HP. Byblos already knows this ability when he joins Ramza's company.

Damage from Soulbind is fixed and unavoidable.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

The Gil Toss move used by Jugglers deals 30 damage by spending 30 gil. Templars can use Lifebreak learned for 300 AP from the Kain's Lance, and deals damage equal to the HP lost. It is Dark elemental, and thus the damage will be reversed on undead enemies. The range of the ability is equal to the weapon currently equipped.

Snipers can use Doom Archer. However, Doom Archer's damage is split between HP and MP.

#### *Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift*

Lifebreak deal dark damage equal to the amount of HP the user has lost. It can be learned from Kain's Lance for 400 AP by the Templar.

### *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius*

Fixed Damage was represented as 1 of 4 damage types, along with Physical, Magic, and Hybrid.

10 Needles was featured as a joke move of sorts.

### *Final Fantasy Dimensions II*

Revenge Blast is the summon attack of Wrieg's Kaiser Dragon Eidolon. It deals neutral-elemental damage based on Wrieg's current HP and lowers the attack, speed, and magic of all enemies for three turns.

### *Final Fantasy: The 4 Heroes of Light*

Eye for an Eye is the level 3 ability of the Dark Fencer class. At the cost of 3 AP, Ruby x2, Emerald x6, Topaz x6, and Amethyst x1, the user will deal damage to one target equal to the difference in their Max HP and current HP.
