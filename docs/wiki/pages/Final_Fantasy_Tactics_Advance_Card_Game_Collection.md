# Final Fantasy Tactics Advance Card Game Collection

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Fantasy_Tactics_Advance_Card_Game_Collection
> Group: Core

*Card back*

Final Fantasy Tactics Advance Card Game Collection (CGC) released in 2003 by Amada Printing.

All cards relate to Final Fantasy Tactics Advance and were only available in Japan.

There are a total of 80 single-sided cards split into three categories:

- 34 x Character Cards
- 40 x Ability Cards
- 6 x Law Cards
