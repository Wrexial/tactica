# Jelly (Tactics Advance)

>

> Description

The Jelly (レッドマシュマロ, Reddomashumaro?, lit. Red Marshmallow) in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a plasma-like monster that is related to the Cream and Ice Flan. It follows the Flan mold of having a huge Defense stat, but being very weak to magical spells. Jelly absorbs Fire damage, but can be destroyed easily with nearly any other elemental spell.

See also: Red Marshmallow
