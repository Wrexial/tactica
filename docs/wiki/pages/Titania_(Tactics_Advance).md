> Description

The Titania in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a powerful enemy that belies its elegant appearance. Its Attack power is high, even surpassing Malboro and Red Panther. Its Holy-elemental spells protect its allies and decimate its enemies. Titania has Auto-Float status.

## Etymology

Titania is a character in William Shakespeare's play A Midsummer Night's Dream. The name "Titania" means "giant, great one", and is derived from Ovid's Metamorphoses, where it is an appellation given to Titan's daughters. In A Midsummer Night's Dream, Titania is the queen of the fairies.
