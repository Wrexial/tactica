> Description

The Sprite in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a fairy-type monster who has the Float ability and absorbs Holy-based attacks. Sprite has low Attack and Defense, but high Magic Resistance and Magic Power. Its White Wind skill is a powerful healing tool, so the player should eliminate it with a strong physical attack in one shot.
