# Final Fantasy Tactics Advance allusions

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Fantasy_Tactics_Advance_allusions
> Group: Core

List of references Final Fantasy Tactics Advance makes to previous Final Fantasy titles and other media.

## *Final Fantasy* series

- Biggs and Wedge appear at the beginning of the game as subordinates of Cid Randell. Their names can later appear as random units.
- The mission "Falcon Flown" is about a falcon named Hyperion who has gone missing, referring either the recurring Chocobo or weapon.

### *Final Fantasy*

- A series of missions where one acquires the sigil mission items has the players fight monsters named after the Four Fiends: Lich, Marilith, Kraken, and Tiamat. The sigils themselves are referenced to the four crystals.
- The Rat Tail mission item is a reference to the rat's tail, the item the player gives to Bahamut to upgrade their jobs.

### *Final Fantasy II*

- The Mindu Gem accessory is a reference to Minwu.
- The Mysidia Alloy and the mission "Mysidia Magic" is a reference to Mysidia, the recurring town of mages.

### *Final Fantasy III*

- The Onion Sword is a reference to the Onion Knight job, and is said to be order-made for the "legendary Onion Knight".

### *Final Fantasy IV*

- An antagonistic clan known as the Red Wings can be fought as a series of side quests in the game. This is a reference to Baron's Red Wings airship force, of which Cecil Harvey was the commander of.
  - Three members of the Red Wings call themselves the Magus Sisters, bosses who originated from Final Fantasy IV.
  - Another member named Grissom is also called a Dark Knight. Although the job is not available in the game, it references how Cecil was a Dark Knight when he lead the Red Wings.

### *Final Fantasy V*

- The missions "Blade & Turtle" and "Armor & Turtle" are posted by a blacksmith named Gilgame. Gilgame was the original translation of the Gil Turtle in the PlayStation release of Final Fantasy V.

### *Final Fantasy VII*

- The Buster Sword is a reference to the iconic sword from the game.

### *Final Fantasy VIII*

- The Lionheart sword is a reference to Squall Leonhart's ultimate weapon.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics*

- The Materia Blade is a reference to the blade of the same name from Tactics, which in turn is a reference to Final Fantasy VII.
- The Rukavi Soul is a reference to the Lucavi, the main threats of the game, although the name is mistranslated.
- The mission "Secret Books" has an anonymous poster requesting help as they have books proving the Selbaden Church's shady dealings. This may be a reference to the Scriptures of Germonique and the Durai Papers, which exposed the corruption of the Church of Glabados in the game.
- There are two references to Messam Elmdore: in the quest "Karlos' Day", his name is mentioned in the description of the quest in the pub. There is also an engagement involving a Bangaa Templar named Silverlock.
  - In the same engagement, there are two Viera Assassins named Celia and Redy.
- The Zodiac Ore mission item is a reference to the Zodiac Stones.

### *Final Fantasy: Unlimited*

- The Calling Gun is a reference to the Magun, and is described as a lost gun of old once used to summon fabulous beasts.

## Other games

### *The Legend of Zelda* series

- The Master Sword is a reference to the iconic recurring sword from the series.

### *Mortal Kombat* series

- The mission "Shadow Clan" is a challenge by the clan's boss, Hanzo. This could be a reference to Scorpion's human form, Hanzo Hasashi, who is the leader of the Shirai Ryu clan of ninjas.
