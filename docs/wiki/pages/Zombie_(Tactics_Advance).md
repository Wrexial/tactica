> Description

Zombies are undead enemies in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. As such, they are weak to Fire and Cure spells, and will revive themselves three turns after being Knocked Out. They are less dangerous than the other undead enemy, the Vampire, having no teleportation abilities and less-than-spectacular HP and movement stats. As they are in permanent Zombie status, burying skills will remove them from battle.

## Etymology

A zombie is a being created through the reanimation of a cadaver, a corporeal manifestation of the revenant type. The term comes from Haitian folklore (zonbi), in which a zombie is a dead body reanimated through necromantic magic by a bokor sorcerer (a voodoo priest). Reports of undead creatures similar to zombies can be traced back to ancient Mesopotamia, in texts such as Descent of Ishtar and Epic of Gilgamesh. Since the late 19th century, zombies have acquired popularity in North American and European folklore.

The most common interpretation and archetype of the zombie, as an undead person that attacks and eats the flesh of living people, is drawn largely from George A. Romero's 1968 film Night of the Living Dead, which in turn was partly inspired by Richard Matheson's 1954 novel I Am Legend. While the word "zombie" is not used in Night of the Living Dead, it was later attributed by fans.

In popular culture, zombies frequently appear in horror genre works, such as movies and video-games. Modern media depictions of the reanimation of the dead often do not involve magic but rather science fictional methods such as fungi, radiation, gases, infection, diseases, plants, bacteria, viruses, etc.
