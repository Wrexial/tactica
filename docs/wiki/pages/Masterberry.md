# Masterberry

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Masterberry
> Group: Enemies

> Purple, hooded monster. Carries a lantern.
>
>
> Description

The Masterberry in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a purple variation of the Tonberry. It is even more vicious than its green cousin, mainly because of its high Speed and devastating Voodoo attack.

## Abilities

### Malice

> Masterberry command. Attack with utter malice.
>
>
> Description

| Ability | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Knife | Tonberry knife stab. Reduces HP to 1/10. |
| Voodoo | This attack carries a powerful, deadly curse.<br>Deals more than 900 damage. |
