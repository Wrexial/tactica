> Description

Deti Plains (ディーテ平原, Dīte heigen?) is a location in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. It can be placed after completing "Mission #021: Hidden Vein" and is the site of "Mission #022: To Ambervale". In this mission, Judgemaster Cid asks Marche to meet him at Deti Plains, however, a horde of monsters must be cleared out of the area first.

After the battle, Cid offers his help in working with Ezel Berbier in making the ultimate Antilaw. Deti Plains is also the site of "Mission #059: Sketchy Thief", "Mission #067: Missing Meow", and "Mission #094: Sorry, Friend". It can be freed in "Mission #257: Deti Plains" after "Mission #022: To Ambervale" is completed.
