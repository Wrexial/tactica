# Coeurl (Tactics Advance)

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Coeurl_%28Tactics_Advance%29
> Group: Enemies

> King of the red panthers. Turns prey to stone.
>
>
> Description

The Coeurl in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a powerful blue beast related to the Red Panther. It has high HP and is dangerous to approach because of its Blaster skill and high Attack power. There is usually never more than one Coeurl at a time.

## Etymology

The coeurl is a fictional alien race created by science-fiction writer A. E. van Vogt. Coeurls also appear in other fantastical environments, such as in Dungeons & Dragons (as "Displacer Beasts"). Coeurls are generally described to be feline with longer forelimbs and tentacles.
