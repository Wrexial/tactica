# Jagd

> Group: Core

>

> Vaan to Penelo

A jagd (ヤクト, yakuto?) is a piece of land that works outside the norms of Ivalice in Final Fantasy XII, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, and Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift. In Final Fantasy XII, normal skystones lose their effect when in jagd territory, making it impossible for airships that run on skystones to cross it.

In Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, jagds are zones in which judges do not appear. As such, there are no laws in jagds and the lack of judges also means that characters are able to permanently die, if not revived before the battle's end.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy XII*

See also: Sage Knowledge 27-52 § SK 38: Jagd

Skystones do not work properly when crossing the border into jagds due to the large amount of ambient Mist, as well as the rich amount of magicite in jagd soil, which interfere with the skystone. No country has been interested in controlling jagds, making them lawless. Some minority races make their societies on jagd terrain, such as Urutan-Yensa on the sandseas and the Baknamy of the Nabreus Deadlands.

Using manufacted nethicite, the people of Archadia have made skystones that can fly over jagds and reach areas like the Tomb of Raithwall and the Ridorana Cataract by airship. The jagd territories seen in the game are:

- Jagd Difohr
- Jagd Naldoa
- Jagd Ramooda
- Jagd Yensa

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

*A Jagd.*

Jagds are the three lawless zones: Jagd Dorsa, Jagd Ahli, and Jagd Helje. Any battle fought at a jagd will not be subjected to the laws of Ivalice, and thus heroes do not have to worry about getting yellow cards or red cards. Since there is no judge present, any character killed at a jagd who is not resurrected before the battle's end is permanently killed.

The player cannot flee from a jagd. Because Marche is the clan leader, and already forbidden to acquire a red card, if Marche passes away in a jagd, it is Game Over. Montblanc can die without a Game Over and there will be some dialogue of Marche discovering Montblanc is dead. Montblanc's role will then be filled in by other clan members, typically Ezel.

#### Unlocking requirements

Jagds do not necessarily need to be unlocked in this order, as the missions needed to unlock them do not necessarily appear in such order.

| Jagd | Unlock condition |
| --- | --- |
| Jagd Dorsa | After completing "#010 Pale Company", Baguba Port is placed on the map. Upon landing on Baguba Port, a cutscene occurs. Afterwards, Jagd Dorsa becomes available. |
| Jagd Ahli | After completing "#016 The Big Find", the "#065 Exploration" side mission is unlocked. Afterwards, Jagd Ahli becomes available. |
| Jagd Helje | After completing "#020 Present Day", the "#057 Prison Break" side mission will be unlocked. Upon completion, the Helje Key is rewarded. Using key, the player can complete "#064 Den of Evil". Afterwards, Jagd Helje become available. |

#### Liberating the jagds

Despite them being unlocked early enough in the game, jagds are unable to be liberated until after the storyline is completed.

When sending a unit out on a dispatch mission, the conditions for completion is beating 15 enemies. After the completion of the storyline, and unlocking all of the turfs, the time allowed to defend an area drops to 30 days. Since jagds can't be liberated until after the storyline has been completed, they won't ever have a 40 day limit like before.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift*

Only one jagd is seen in Jylland, Zellea, the Forbidden Land. Here, the mist is strong and unstable, so unstable that rifts have a tendency to open up all the time. These rifts have greatly scarred the landscape.

## Other media

The Beverage Shop in Luxerion in Lightning Returns: Final Fantasy XIII sells Jagd Frozen Yogurt. Also, there is a forest in the Wildlands called Jagd Woods with Jagd Village within.

## Etymology

Jagd is the German word for the noun "hunt".
