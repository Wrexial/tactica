The following is a list of the accessories present in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance.

## Shoes

Shoes add very little to defense and resistance comparatively, but have the largest increases of evade, jump, and movement. They can be equipped by any job, but do not teach any abilities. Shoes also are the only inventory item unable to be stolen.
