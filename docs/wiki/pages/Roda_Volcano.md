# Roda Volcano

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Roda_Volcano
> Group: Locations

Roda Volcano is a location in the world of Ivalice. It is an active volcano, and many strange things have happened there.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy XII: Revenant Wings*

Main article: Feol Warren

Roda Volcano is home to the feol viera, a sub-branch of the viera banished for being half-bloods. The party goes to Roda Volcano to rescue the feol viera from the Judge of Wings, but are too late to stop her.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

Main article: Roda Volcano (Tactics Advance)

Roda Volcano is a mountain location, and is the only place in Ivalice besides Tubola Cave where lava flows. The Totema Ultima is battled here.

## Etymology

The word 'Roda' means 'wheel' in Portuguese, Malay and Indonesian.
