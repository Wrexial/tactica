> Description

The Blade Biter in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a purple turtle-like monster with extremely high Defense and high Attack stats. Being a stronger version of normal Toughskin, it has the capability to permanently destroy weapons and unleash a devastatingly powerful attack when at critically low HP. Melee attackers should take care not to get their weapons crushed, especially if they are rare or one of a kind. Like its lesser cousin, it also possesses the Resonate ability to further decrease the usefulness of most physical weapons.

Unlike most monsters, the Blade Biter has no abilities that can be learned as Blue Magic.
