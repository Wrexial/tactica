>
>
> Description

The Ice Flan is an enemy in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance that is related to the Cream and the Jelly. Like most jellies in the Final Fantasy series, its Defense is phenomenally high, but it is extremely weak to certain elemental spells. The Ice Flan absorbs Ice. It is advisable to use Lightning, Water, Wind, Earth, Holy, and especially Fire-based spells to defeat it.

## Etymology

The word Flan refers to an open pastry or sponge cake containing a sweet or savory filling. A typical flan of this sort is round, with shortcrust pastry, usually coated with sweet syrup. It is similar to a custard tart or a South African melktert.
