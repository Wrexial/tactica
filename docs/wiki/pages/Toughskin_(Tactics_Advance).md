> Description

The Toughskin in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is a green turtle-like creature with a sword sticking out of its back. It has an extremely high Defense stat, so magic spells are much more effective compared to physical attacks. Its Resonance ability can also disable the weapons used by most physically orientated jobs, further necessitating the use of magic.

Toughskins can be dangerous against physical units with low MP, as it can use Matra Magic to invert the HP and MP of a target. To avoid this attack, it is best to keep a distance by using long-range bows and magic. Matra Magic can also be learned by the Blue Mage job as a Blue Magic ability. The Toughskin has an even hardier lavender cousin, the Blade Biter.
