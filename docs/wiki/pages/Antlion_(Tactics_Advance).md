> Description

The Antlion is a blue insect in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, and unlike some previous games, a common enemy rather a boss. It makes use of its strong mandibles and affinity with sand to damage party members. It has a stronger relative called Jawbreakers.

Its LVL 3 Def-Less is learnable as a Blue Magic ability; the Blue Mage needs to be on a level that is a multiple of 3 to learn it. Since the maximum level is 50, LVL 3 Def-Less can become missable after a unit passes level 48. LVL 3 Def-Less can be useful when an enemy's level is just right, since it always works and it has an area effect.

The Antlion's other ability, Sandstorm, is usable by Morphers since they are capturable. Morphers can use Antlion abilities with Jawbreaker abilities if the player has one. Sandstorm provides damage while also blinding enemies, severely decreasing the accuracy on all of their attacks. Sandstorm's damage output can always be increased by feeding the monster.

## Etymology

Antlions are a family of insects, known by the scientific name Myrmeleontidae. "Antlion" usually refers to the larval form of the insect, and they are so named because the larva's typical food is ants, though they will eat other insects as well. Antlions will dig pits in sand inches deep and lurk in the bottom waiting for prey to come close. When the prey falls into the pit, the loose sand on the sides of the pit prevents them from escaping and causes them to fall further down towards the antlion. The antlion's jaws are hollow, and upon biting its prey it will suck out its juices rather than actually eat it. When the antlion is finished, it flicks the husk out of its sandpit and fixes any damage done to the walls, then waits for its next meal to pass by.

Ant-lion, or Myrmecoleon, also refers to a large mythical creature that bears the features of both ants and lions. In some renditions, it is instead portrayed as a beast that consumes ants.
