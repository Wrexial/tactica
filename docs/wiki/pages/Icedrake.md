# Icedrake

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Icedrake
> Group: Enemies

> Giant scaly monster. Breathes clouds of ice.
>
>
> Description

The Icedrake in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance is the Ice variant of the powerful dragon family. Being the more defensively oriented dragon of the bunch, its Mighty Guard ability raises the Defense and Magic Resistance of itself or an adjacent unit. It tends to use this ability on itself, becoming even more durable. Mighty Guard can be learned by Blue Mages as Blue Magic.
