# Thundrake

> Group: Enemies

> Scaly beast that shoots lightning bolts.
>
>
> Description

The Thundrake is the Lightning-elemental member of the dragon family in Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. Possibly the strongest dragon, its stats completely surpass those of almost all other monsters, and it can boost them even further with its Dragon Force ability. Eventually, it becomes extinct, as there are no repeatable Turf Defense missions or clans that they belong to. Hunters and Dragoons will be helpful, as their abilities have bonus effects against Dragons.

Thundrakes are capturable, and so it should be a top priority before they go "extinct". This gives Morphers access to its Dragon Force ability along with Bolt Breath. Thundrake abilities can be used with other abilities from Firewyrms and Icedrakes. Due to a Thundrake's naturally superior stats, they can be maxed out easily allowing Morphers to deal blistering damage with Bolt Breath. Dragon Force can also be learned by Blue Mages as Blue Magic.

### Dragonbolt

>
> Description

| Ability | Effect | MP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dragon Force | Releases the dragon soul within. All stats improve. | 12 |
| Bolt Breath | Electrically charged cloud. Deals damage. | — |

Dragon Force is a valuable Blue Magic ability that boosts all stats of one unit. A Beastmaster is required to learn it, as an enemy Thundrake would never use this on a player unit. Thundrake is extremely dangerous if not under control of a Beastmaster and the player should take care that the Blue Mage in question does not get killed by the Thundrake during the learning process.
