# Reaction ability

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Reaction_ability
> Group: Abilities

Reaction abilities (リアクションアビリティ, Riakushon Abiritei?) are abilities that require a trigger to be activated, and once activated, their effects take place.

## Appearances

### *Final Fantasy Tactics*

Main article: Final Fantasy Tactics reaction abilities

Reaction abilities activate when their trigger condition is met, and the chance for activating also depends on the user's bravery stat in percentage, the braver the higher chance.

### *Final Fantasy Tactics Advance*

Main article: Final Fantasy Tactics Advance reaction abilities

Reaction abilities activate when their trigger condition is met. Unlike its predecessor, the reaction abilities varies, ranging from retaliating actions to bestowing positive status.
