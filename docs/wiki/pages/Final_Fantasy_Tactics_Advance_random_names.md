# Final Fantasy Tactics Advance random names

> Source: https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Final_Fantasy_Tactics_Advance_random_names
> Group: Core

In Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, all units that appear in-battle are given names, which are chosen at random from three lists. Two units of the same name never appear together in one battle. The following is a list of all the possible names that can be given by the system.

In the English version, the first members of Clan Nutsy (excluding Marche Radiuju and Montblanc) will receive random names. However in the Japanese version, their names are Makenro (nu mou), Karorin (viera), Mōni (bangaa), and Emeto (human). They are names of the members of Clan Centurio.

## Collections

While the names of units are randomly generated, it is possible, though time-consuming, to try to collect units with certain names that go together, as many of the names are drawn from or are similar to names in literature, film, and history. This is purely for aesthetics, as a unit's name has no bearing on its stats. Some collections worth noting are:

***Ghost Busters***

- Benkman (Peter Venkman)
- Stanz (Ray Stantz)
- Spengler (Egon Spengler)
- Dana (Dana Barrett)
- Louis (Louis Tully)

***Les Miserables***

- Miserie (Title)
- Eponine (Eponine Thenardier)
- Marius (Marius Pontmercy)
- Javet (Inspector Javert)

***Pride and Prejudice***

- Bingley (Charles Bingley)
- Darcy (Fitzwilliam Darcy)
- Caroline (Caroline Bingley)
- Georg (George Wickham)
- Catherine (Catherine Bennett, Lady Catherine du Bourgh)
- Lucas (Charlotte Lucas)

**Roman rulers**

- Cesare (Julius Caesar)
- Augusto (Augustus)
- Nero (Nero)
- Tonio (Antonius Pius)
- Aurelie (Marcus Aurelius, Aurelian)
- Julian (Julius Caesar, Didius Julianus, Julian I, Julian II)
- Phil (Philip I, Philip II)
- Flora (Florian)
- Johanes (Joannes)
- Thedoric (Theodoric the Great - Ostrogoth who conquered Rome, ended western dynasty)
- Leo (Leo I, Leo II - eastern dynastay)
